Fuel Density Calculation is an iOS app available on the Apple App Store.

About

Hate mental math? This app is designed for wide-body aircrews (Boeing & Airbus) to compare fuel calculations—incorporating density—between uploaded (uplifted) and actual fuel on board (FOB).

Privacy Policy

Data Collection

The Fuel Density Calculation app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal user data. We do not access your contacts, location, or any other information on your device.
